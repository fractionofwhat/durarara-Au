# Durarara!! AU – Story Bible

**Working Title:** Curtis in Ikebukuro  
**Status:** Ongoing collaborative long-form AU  
**Setting:** Aged-up Durarara!! (characters 18–19, Raira is a college)

This repository is the central continuity and reference document for the Durarara!! alternate universe centered on the original character **Curtis Nightstar**.

## Purpose of This Story Bible

This repo exists to preserve continuity across chapters, model upgrades, and long writing sessions. It tracks:

- Character profiles and relationships
- Timeline of events (chapter-by-chapter)
- Revealed vs. unrevealed information (who knows what)
- Curtis’s abilities, background, and dual identities
- Key locations, items, and ongoing plot threads
- Canon divergences and AU rules

Because this story is written iteratively and over a long period, this bible is the single source of truth for what has already happened and what each character currently knows.

## Core Premise

Curtis Nightstar, a 6'3" First Nations Canadian from the Northwest Territories with a complicated and violent past, arrives in Ikebukuro.  
He is multilingual, abnormally durable, and carries both ordinary and supernatural scars.  
While trying to live quietly as a college student at Raira, he is repeatedly pulled into the city’s shadows — Celty, Izaya, Shizuo, the Dollars, Saika, and the personal lives of those around him.

The story follows both his public life (student, class representative, occasional protector) and his hidden one (masked/night rider, white-smoke abilities, unfinished list, and the weight of what he left behind in Appalachia).

## How to Use This Repo

- Check the relevant character or timeline file before writing new scenes
- Update information logs after major reveals
- Keep “Who Knows What” sections accurate — information is highly compartmentalized
- Record new abilities, items, or relationship shifts as they appear

## Notes

- Characters are aged up to 18–19. Raira Academy is treated as a college.
- Celty’s voice rules follow canon except for the private exception with Curtis.
- Curtis’s dual identities (school version / night versions) are not fully known to most characters.
- This is a living document and will be updated as the story progresses.
